Privacy Policy

RideApp Tanzania (rider app) · Last updated 18 September 2026

This policy explains what RideApp Tanzania collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers the Android rider app (tz.rideapp.rider) and its backend services.

What we collect

DataWhy
Phone numberTo create your account and sign you in by one-time code. This is the only identifier required.
Display nameShown to your driver so they can identify you at pickup.
Precise locationTo set your pickup point, show nearby vehicles, calculate fares and routes, and track the trip in progress. Collected while the app is in use.
Trip recordsPickup and drop-off points, distance, duration, fare and status — kept so you have a ride history and for support, safety and billing disputes.
Saved placesShortcuts you choose to save, stored on your account.
Ratings and commentsFeedback you leave after a trip, used for driver quality and safety.
Push notification tokenIf you enable notifications, to tell you when a driver accepts, arrives or completes your trip.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We show no advertising and embed no advertising SDKs.
  • We use no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not store full card numbers. Payments are handled by regulated mobile-money and card processors; we keep only the method you chose and the transaction reference.
  • We do not track your location when the app is closed.

Who your data is shared with

  • Your driver — your first name, pickup and drop-off points, and live position during the trip.
  • Payment processors — the amount and reference needed to take payment.
  • Our hosting provider (Supabase) — which stores the data on our behalf under contract.
  • Authorities — only where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

Account details are kept while your account is open. Trip records are retained for seven years to meet tax and transport-regulation requirements. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and contact details and retain only the anonymised trip records we are legally obliged to keep.

Your choices

  • Location permission can be withdrawn at any time in Android Settings; the app cannot book a ride without it.
  • Notifications can be turned off in Android Settings.
  • You can request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing us.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Session tokens are held in the Android encrypted keystore on your device. Access to the production database is restricted and governed by row-level security so one rider cannot read another's records.

Children

RideApp Tanzania is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and notify you in the app.
