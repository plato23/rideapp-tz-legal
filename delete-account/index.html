Delete your account

RideApp Tanzania (tz.rideapp.rider) · Last updated 18 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your RideApp Tanzania account and the data attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

From inside the app

  1. Open RideApp Tanzania and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

By email

Send a request to maxwellplato@gmail.com from the phone number on your account, or include that number in the message so we can identify you. We respond within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

DataWhat happens
Your phone number and display nameDeleted
Saved places and payment preferenceDeleted
Push notification tokensDeleted
Ratings and comments you leftDeleted, or detached from your identity where a driver's overall score depends on them
Completed trip recordsKept for seven years in anonymised form — stripped of your name and phone number — because tax and transport regulations require it

Once deletion is complete you will not be able to sign back into the account, and we cannot restore it. You are welcome to create a new account afterwards.

Questions

Email maxwellplato@gmail.com, or read our privacy policy.
